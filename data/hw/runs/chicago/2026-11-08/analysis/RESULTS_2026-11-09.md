## 2. The centre collapsed on the 8 Nov layout

The centre kappa, the quantity W00 rests on, is 0.31 / -0.06 / +0.23
(control) and 0.52 / 0.03 / 0.04 (eps05) at t = 2 / 4 / 6, against
0.80 / 0.67 / 0.55 in production. Statistical errors at the centre are
0.004, so the non-monotonic pattern is real; it looks coherent, not
decoherence. The physics circuits at the centre are only mildly worse
(damping 0.45 / 0.44 / 0.40 vs 0.56 / 0.57 / 0.61), and the t = 0 reference
at the centre is as good as in production (0.90 vs 0.90). The mirror itself
is what fails.

Cause: the embedding chooser picked the same 101-qubit region as 6 Nov but
traversed in the opposite direction (3 dead qubits instead of 4 that day).
The centre landed on q108, which on 6 Nov was the failing odd site 25
(kappa -0.03 / -0.05 / -0.33). q108 is the worst qubit in the neighbourhood
in the 8 Nov calibration snapshot: T2 = 70 us (neighbours 170 to 320 us,
except q106 at 22 us and q109 at 82 us), and its four couplers carry the
highest rzz errors nearby (108-109 0.0043, 108-98 0.0040, 108-107 0.0032,
108-118 0.0028, against 0.0016 to 0.0024 elsewhere). The 6 Nov centre
qubit q107 (couplers 0.0019 to 0.0020 plus 0.0032 to q108) is site 25 on
8 Nov with kappa 0.32 / 0.19 / 0.11, so a good centre qubit does not make
a good odd site either: neither the physical qubit alone nor the logical
site alone predicts the mirror kappa. Only 31 of the 50 matter qubits are
shared between the two layouts and only four of those have a usable t = 0
signal in both roles, so no correlation statement beyond this is possible.

Physical chain around the insertion (site: qubit; link qubits between):

    6 Nov  21:95  22:105  23:106  24:107  25:108  26:109  27:99   ancilla 117 (on 107)
    8 Nov  21:78  22:88   23:98   24:108  25:107  26:106  27:96   ancilla 118 (on 108)

Ancilla X bias: +0.10 on q117 (6 Nov), +0.02 on q118 (8 Nov): the bias is a
property of the ancilla qubit, which supports de-biasing per run.

## 3. Mirror return of the matter Z profile

RMS over sites of <Z_n>(mirror) - <Z_n>(t=0 ref): production 0.19 / 0.35 /
0.45, control 0.23 / 0.31 / 0.35, eps05 0.19 / 0.28 / 0.34 at t = 2 / 4 / 6,
against physics - ref 0.28 to 0.41. The mirrors return the profile only
marginally better than the physics circuits move it; this is the depth
damping of the staggered profile, equal in both runs. It does not single
out the centre.

## Consequences

- The 8 Nov control is not a replication of 6 Nov: it is a different layout
  with a worse centre qubit. Variant-vs-control comparisons within 8 Nov
  are valid (same layout, 70 s apart); 8 Nov vs 6 Nov comparisons are
  layout-dominated.
- Any J1-only or W00 rerun on Chicago must not put the centre on q108.
  `replay_embedding.py` rebuilds the chooser's input from Declan's saved
  calibration (plus the one CZ cut the rzz-only snapshot cannot see) and
  reproduces the eight candidate ladders, the recorded layout among them,
  with scores 1.219 to 1.245: the insertion site is 1/200 of the score, so
  the choice was effectively a coin toss. Fix (9 Nov, Henry's go-ahead):
  `--min-t2 US` rejects candidates whose Hadamard-test core (site 24, its
  two neighbours, their links, the ancilla) has a qubit with T2 below US;
  at 100 us the replay selects site 24 on q88 (T2 193 us, ancilla q89 298
  us, core min 125 us) and rejects six of the eight. A device-wide floor is
  not viable: 100 us removes 30 of 120 qubits and no Ns=50 ladder survives.
  The core T2 is recorded in every acceptance record from now on.
- The twirled variant (`--pre-twirl 4`) must be run back to back with a
  fresh control on the same layout, and the layout (centre qubit, ancilla
  qubit) reported, otherwise the comparison is meaningless.
- For the talk and paper: the Chicago W00 replication of 6 Nov stands (that
  run had a good centre). The J1 components remain unmeasured on Chicago.
